      how it works

      public sources in, a CRM-ready list out.

      Everything here comes from pages anyone can open: issuer press releases, ETF news roundups, exchange listing notices, the BlossomCon sponsor page and posts where Blossom's own team named its clients. Each launch links back to the page it came from.

      1. Collect

        Pull every ETF that started trading in the window, with its ticker, issuer, date, category and source link.

      2. Match

        Normalize issuer names, then flag issuers that Blossom's team has named as partners or that already appear in the app.

      3. Score

        Weigh launch count, recency, fit with Blossom's audience and the US push. The sliders above change the weights live.

      4. Export

        Copy the ranked list as CSV and paste it into the CRM, one row per issuer with the suggested pitch.

      What "fit" means

      Blossom's careers page describes its members as long-term investors who want to "get rich slow", and the app's main topics are ETFs, Passive Income, Dividends and Beginner Investors. Products that match those topics score higher. Leveraged single-stock funds score lowest because they suit short-term traders.

      Already working with Blossom
